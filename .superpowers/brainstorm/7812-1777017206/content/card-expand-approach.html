Clicking an image scrolls up and inserts a full-width MultiCard at the top. The masonry grid continues below with the selected image highlighted. Clean separation — card and grid coexist.

B — Inline expansion, grid reflows

The card expands in-place using column-span: all in the CSS columns masonry. The clicked item disappears and the card appears in the flow. Other items reflow around it. Most "in-grid" feeling but complex to animate.

The card appears as a modal-like overlay that covers the grid (within its container — not full screen). The grid dims behind it. Easiest to animate with Framer Motion layoutId. Similar to Apple's app store expand pattern.
